ALGORITHM 1: CVSS v3.1-INSPIRED SCORER (0-10)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:  Findings with severity, confidence, context
        
Process:
        Base Score
        [Critical: 9.0, High: 7.0, Medium: 5.0, Low: 3.0]
              │
              ▼
        Environmental Factors (Multiplier)
        [Auth: 1.4x, Production: 1.3x, Admin: 1.5x, Test: 0.3x]
              │
              ▼
        Confidence Adjustment (0-1)
        [0.98 = 98% certain]
              │
              ▼
        Final Score = Base × EnvFactor × Confidence
        Capped at: 10.0 max
        
Output: CVSS Score (0-10) + Breakdown by severity

Example:
        Plaintext password in auth.py
        Base: 9.0 × (1.4 × 1.3) × 0.98 = 15.98 → 10.0

═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ALGORITHM 2: WEIGHTED AGGREGATE SCORER (0-100)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:  Findings with severity, confidence
        
Process:
        Start: 100 (Perfect)
              │
              ▼
        Calculate Weights:
        [Critical: -40, High: -25, Medium: -12, Low: -5, Info: -1]
              │
              ▼
        Apply Confidence (Multiply by 0-1):
        [Weight × Confidence per finding]
              │
              ▼
        Total Weighted Points
              │
              ▼
        Reduction Factor × 0.8
              │
              ▼
        Score = 100 - (Total × 0.8)
        Clamped: 0-100
        
Output: Weighted Score (0-100) + Total weight breakdown

Example:
        1 Critical (0.95): 40 × 0.95 = 38
        2 High (0.85):    25 × 0.85 × 2 = 42.5
        Total: 80.5 × 0.8 = 64.4
        Score: 100 - 64.4 = 35.6

ALGORITHM 3: RISK LEVEL CATEGORICAL SCORER (Critical/High/Medium/Low/Info)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:  Finding counts by severity
        
Process:
        Count by Severity
        [critical=?, high=?, medium=?, low=?]
              │
              ▼
        Apply Thresholds:
        ┌─ Critical ≥ 1 → CRITICAL 🔴
        ├─ High ≥ 3 → HIGH 🟠
        ├─ Medium ≥ 10 → MEDIUM 🟡
        ├─ Low > 0 → LOW 🟢
        └─ Else → INFO ⚪
              │
              ▼
        Assign Risk Score (1-10)
        Provide Reasoning
        Suggest Color Coding
        
Output: Risk Level + Score + Reasoning + Color

Example:
        1 critical → CRITICAL (10) → "Fix immediately"

═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ALGORITHM 4: CONFIDENCE-WEIGHTED SCORER (0-100)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:  Findings with confidence, severity
        
Process:
        Calculate Average Confidence
        [Sum of all confidences / count]
        Example: (0.98 + 0.85 + 0.65) / 3 = 0.826
              │
              ▼
        Severity to Weight Mapping
        [Critical: 50, High: 30, Medium: 15, Low: 5]
              │
              ▼
        Calculate Severity Sum
        [Sum of mapped weights]
              │
              ▼
        Apply Confidence Factor
        Score = 100 - (Sum × (1 - AvgConfidence))
        Example: 100 - (95 × (1 - 0.826))
               = 100 - (95 × 0.174) = 100 - 16.5 = 83.5
              │
              ▼
        Identify Low-Confidence Findings (<0.7)
        
Output: Confidence Score (0-100) + Average confidence + Low-conf count

═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ALGORITHM 5: ENTROPY RISK SCORER (0-100 + Distribution)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:  Findings with file names
        
Process:
        Build File Distribution
        {
          "auth.py": 15 findings,
          "db.py": 2 findings,
          "utils.py": 3 findings
        }
              │
              ▼
        Calculate Shannon Entropy
        entropy = -Σ(p_i × log2(p_i))
        where p_i = count_i / total
              │
              ▼
        Normalize Entropy
        [Divide by max_entropy = log2(num_files)]
        Result: 0-1 (0=concentrated, 1=spread)
              │
              ▼
        Convert to Score (0-100)
        Classify Concentration:
        ┌─ High (entropy < 0.3): Concentrated
        ├─ Medium (0.3-0.7): Moderate
        └─ Low (entropy > 0.7): Spread
              │
              ▼
        Calculate Risk Reduction Factor
        [1.0x for concentrated, 0.8x for spread]
        
Output: Entropy Score + Concentration Level + Files Affected + Risk Factor

Example:
        15 findings in 2 files / 20 total
        Proportion: 0.75 and 0.1
        Entropy: -(0.75×log2(0.75) + 0.1×log2(0.1)) ≈ 0.82
        Normalized: 0.82/1.0 = 0.82 → 82 (LOW concentration)

ALGORITHM SYNTHESIS: PRIMARY SCORE CALCULATION
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

        Primary score uses finding severity counts only:
        Score = clamp(100 - (critical × 40 + high × 25 + medium × 12
                             + low × 5), 0, 100)
        Informational findings do not reduce the score.
        Confidence, file context, and supplementary scorers do not affect it.

        Example counts:
        1 critical + 1 high + 1 medium + 1 low + any info
        Deductions = 40 + 25 + 12 + 5 = 82
        Score = 100 - 82 = 18 / 100
